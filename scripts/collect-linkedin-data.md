# Guide to Collecting Real LinkedIn Recruiter Data

Since I cannot directly access LinkedIn profiles, here's how you can collect real recruiter data:

## Method 1: Manual Collection (Recommended)

1. Go to LinkedIn and search for "Technical Recruiter" in each country
2. Filter by location (US, UK, Canada, Australia, India)
3. For each recruiter:
   - Copy their LinkedIn profile URL (e.g., `https://www.linkedin.com/in/username/`)
   - Note their name, company, and specializations
   - Copy their bio/headline
   - Note their experience level

## Method 3: Using LinkedIn Sales Navigator (If Available)

If you have LinkedIn Sales Navigator:
1. Search for "Technical Recruiter" or "IT Recruiter"
2. Filter by country
3. Export results or manually collect profile URLs

## Data Format

When collecting data, ensure you have:
- Full name
- LinkedIn profile URL (full URL)
- Current company
- Country
- Specializations (from their profile)
- Years of experience (if mentioned)
- Bio/headline
